Cricket news, here's a cricket news update from India as of June 16, 2025:

Hello cricket fans! Here's the latest from the world of Indian cricket.

First up, Gautam Gambhir is set to rejoin Team India in England on June 17th. He had to rush back home briefly due to a family medical emergency. Assistant coaches were in charge for the intra-squad game, but Gambhir will be back to guide the team ahead of the first Test against England on June 20th.

Speaking of that Test series against England, there's a lot of talk about how the team will shape up with the retirements of Virat Kohli and Rohit Sharma. Newly appointed captain Shubman Gill is facing some tough selection decisions to fill those key spots in the batting lineup.

In other news, veteran India batter Karun Nair revealed that a prominent Indian cricketer advised him to retire and focus on T20 leagues for financial security, this was months before his comeback. Nair is now back in the fold, fighting for a spot in the red-ball team after eight years.

Finally, the schedule for the Women's ODI World Cup 2025 has been announced, and India will face Pakistan on October 5th in Colombo. That's always a highly anticipated match!
